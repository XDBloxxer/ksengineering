Crew manifest

I'm a multi-disciplinary engineer who can't stay in one lane. Mechanical design, structures, electronics and aerospace keep pulling me back in, and the most interesting problems sit where they collide.

I work from first principles: why does it work, not just that it does. Whether it's a load-bearing frame or a PCB, I start the same way. Understand it, build it, break it, fix it.

The long game is spacecraft design: rockets, propulsion, structures that survive launch and live in vacuum. I plan to start right after the Academy, around 2028–29, and everything on this page is practice for that.

Our entry · PLM Innovators Design Contest 2026

The Repair-First Screwdriver

A smart screwdriver that works out the right bit and the right torque, so anyone can fix their own things. Built around EU right-to-repair, and designed to come apart.

  • ScanThe app reads each screw's torque from its repair tag and sends it to the tool.
  • RecogniseAn IR laser probe reads which bit a screw needs. Each magnetic bit has its own wire resistance, so the tool knows what is fitted.
  • Always readyNo screen. USB-C and one Bluetooth button, plus a hand dial for torque on a flat battery.
  • Built to come apartEnd cap, body, motor, PCB and battery are held by screws, not glue.

KMH Group is our initials, and when we work separately, as here, we still put the mark on our work. PCB, battery, motor, clutch and wiring in the model are placeholders.
